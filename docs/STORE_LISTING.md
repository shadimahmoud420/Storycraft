🚀 شارك فوراً

• حفظ التصميم في معرض الصور، أو مشاركته عبر أي تطبيق آخر

🔒 خصوصيتك محفوظة: لا حسابات ولا جمع بيانات ولا إعلانات.

## Full description – English
StoryCraft helps you design beautiful, ready-to-post stories in seconds.

✨ Start your way
• Upload a photo from your gallery
• Pick a classic solid color
• Choose a ready-made gradient, or build your own and set its direction

✍️ Pro typography
• 12+ popular Arabic fonts: Cairo, Tajawal, Almarai, Amiri, Reem Kufi, Ruqaa…
• 12+ popular English fonts: Montserrat, Playfair, Bebas Neue, Pacifico…
• Unlimited colors, size, alignment, text highlight and shadow
• Drag, pinch and rotate. Add as many text blocks as you like

📸 One-tap photo fixes
• Natural: automatic white balance, exposure and contrast
• Enhance: upscales small photos and brings out detail and clarity
• 100% on-device. Your photos never leave your phone

🚀 Share instantly
• Share straight to your Instagram Story in perfect 1080×1920
• Save to Photos or share with any app

🔒 Private by design: no accounts, no data collection, no ads.
